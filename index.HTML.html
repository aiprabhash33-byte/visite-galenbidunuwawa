Who We Are

A village-born community with a island-wide spirit.

From Galenbindunuwewa in the Anuradhapura District, GALENBIDUNUWAWA API brings together friends, families and curious travellers who share a love for mountains, waterfalls, forests, beaches and the stories found along the way.

Every journey is planned as a shared experience: new friendships, responsible exploration, local food, laughter around a campfire and memories that stay with us long after we return home.
